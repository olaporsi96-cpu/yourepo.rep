# The Magic Calculator & The Secret Guard

### What I Learned!
* **My Understanding:** Functions are like tiny, loyal robots. You give them ingredients (numbers), they do a task, and they hand you back a finished product (like a cake!). They never change anything outside of their own little workspace.
* **My Struggles:** It was tricky learning that a robot can take *another* robot with it to help do a job. 
* **My Growth:** Now I know that instead of building one giant, confusing super-robot, I can build lots of tiny, friendly helper robots that work together!
